Sheet 04SpecificationHow we build

How we build

  1. P-01

    Native & measured

    Native Rust with no Node.js or Electron underneath. Hot paths carry Criterion benchmarks, and head-to-head results against tosu are published with their limits stated.

  2. P-02

    Linux first-class

    Native osu!lazer telemetry on Linux, process events over eBPF or CN_PROC, and in-game overlays for Vulkan, OpenGL, Wayland, X11, and Wine. Windows is supported too.

  3. P-03

    Built for power users

    tosu-compatible APIs so existing overlays keep working, plus extensions for people who want more: selected-field streams, per-key timing, and release hit errors.
